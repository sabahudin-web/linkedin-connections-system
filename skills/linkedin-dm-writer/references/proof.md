# The user's proof: case studies and results

DMs never carry proof in message 1, and cold emails carry one proof line at most. Both use ONLY proof the user gives you. This skill ships with no case studies of its own and never borrows anyone else's.

## Ask once, in step 0

"Do you have case studies or client results I can use when a lead asks for an example? Paste links, or a short list: client type, the problem, what changed, and whether you may name them."

Write the answer into a small table in the run notes:

| # | Client (or "anonymous") | Set-up (solo or team) | Problem in their words | What changed (their number) | Link | May name? | Send when they say... |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Rules

1. No proof given means no proof line anywhere. Write the cold email without block 3 and say so in the report.
2. Never invent a client, a number, a quote or a result, and never round a number up.
3. Pick ONE item that matches how the lead is set up (solo or team) first, then the words they used. Never send a list.
4. A client who has not agreed to be named stays anonymous ("a 3-person accounting firm").
5. Proof goes out only when it is the right move: in a cold email's proof line, or in a reply after the lead named a problem, doubted it works or asked for an example (`reply-engine.md`).
