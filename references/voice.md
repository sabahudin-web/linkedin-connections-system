# Writing in the user's voice

The DMs and emails go out under the user's name, so they must sound like the user, not like this skill.

## Before the first message

1. Ask the user for 2 or 3 LinkedIn messages or emails they wrote themselves and liked (ideally ones that got a reply). Match their sentence length, their greeting, their sign-off and how casual they are. Never copy their sentences.
2. If they have none, use the defaults below and say so in the run report.
3. If the user keeps a voice or style file in their own folder, read it and let it win over these defaults.

## Defaults

- Stupidly simple. If a smart 12-year-old would stop on a word, the word is wrong. The outcome in plain words first, tool names after, only if they earn it.
- Say it flat. Prove it with a number. Cut the softener in front of it.
- Short sentences, under about 15 words. One idea, then a full stop.
- Casual, never formal. Talk to a prospect the way you talk to a colleague you like.
- No gendered address (man, mate, sir) unless the person used it first.
- Numerals always (40, not forty). Ranges as "1 to 5". No em dashes or en dashes.

## Banned

- **Corporate words:** leverage, unlock, optimize, transform, seamless, robust, streamline, empower, elevate, holistic, ecosystem, synergy, game-changing, cutting-edge, bandwidth, at scale, best-in-class, revolutionize, skyrocket, supercharge, next-level, value proposition, circle back, touch base, utilize, maximize, end-to-end, ROI, solution, 10x, hustle, quick win.
- **Hedges and filler:** basically, to be honest, I think (for a fact), I mean, just (when it shrinks the work), really, maybe, a little bit, actually, literally, honestly, simply, "The truth is", "As you can see".
- **False intimacy:** "here is what nobody tells you", "Most people think", "Stop X, start Y".
- **The announcement family:** see `dm-floor.md`.
